<objective>
Prove criterion 3 — the three albums appear in Music Assistant under the correct album artist — and
then prove the setting that protects everything after this phase actually works.

Purpose: criterion 3 is where the phase either earns its existence or becomes theatre. Two weaker
forms of this test would pass on a broken system and both are explicitly rejected. "Files are visible"
passes when MA can see a directory. "An album appeared" passes when a `various_artists` or
`folder_name` fallback fired and produced a plausible entry. The only assertion that distinguishes a
correct library from a confidently wrong one is an **exact match** on both the album name and the
album artist, filtered to the provider under test.

The second half matters more for Phase 7 than for this one. `missing_album_artist_action: folder_name`
is currently configured but has never fired. If it ships unverified, its first real exercise is the
Phase 7 pilot at scale — against a library where a wrong album artist creates a durable wrong entry,
because album identity is `albumartist + os.sep + album`. A deliberate control album, on a throwaway
export, is where that gets discovered cheaply.

Output: criterion 3 proven; the fallback proven to fire; both temporary artefacts provably removed.
</objective>

    a compilation whose real album artist happens to contain those words, which is precisely the
    ambiguity this library is full of. Cross-check the artist side with `music/artists/library_items`
    for each expected album artist.

    A wrong answer is diagnostic, and the SUMMARY should say which fallback fired: `Various Artists`
    means the `various_artists` default was somehow in play (which would contradict 02-06's read-back);
    the folder name means the tag was missing and `folder_name` fired; the track artist means
    `track_artist` was configured. Each identifies a different defect.

    **The failure branch (D-05).** If any of the three appears under the wrong album artist, criterion
    3 fails and the phase **halts for diagnosis**. The remedy is **never tag repair**: nobody holds
    `rw` on Music until Phase 6 (Phase 1's D-20) and the export is `ro`, so tag repair is not even
    physically available here. The two legitimate remedies are swapping in a genuinely-correct album —
    recording the reject and why it was wrong, which is itself a useful finding — or fixing MA-side
    configuration. Do not attempt to write to the library under any circumstances.

    The three albums were pre-verified correct in 02-04 against the QUAL-01 snapshot. That is what
    makes a failure here unambiguously MA's or the export's rather than a bad tag, and it is why the
    pre-verification was worth doing first.
