<objective>
Prove CONS-03 — Music Assistant's view survives a NUC reboot — and prove it in the only way that
means anything.

Purpose: a NUC reboot with atlantis healthy **does not exercise the race at all**. The mount simply
succeeds. Criterion 4 as the roadmap words it can therefore pass by luck and prove nothing, which is
why both controls are in scope (D-32).

The failure being tested is specific and was traced through Supervisor's source rather than inferred
from folklore. It is **not** an ordering bug — `sys_mounts.load()` runs before `sys_apps.load()`. The
real failure is that an unreachable server inside the ≤40 s window makes Supervisor bind-mount an
**empty read-only emergency directory** at `/media/<name>`. MA then finds a path that exists and is a
directory, loads normally, scans zero files, and its empty-scan guard preserves the library in a
*stale* state: entries exist, playback of any of them fails. That is the state this phase exists to
prevent, and the only way to know the guards work here is to cause it.

The community reports conflict with current Supervisor source on whether a failed media mount ever
self-recovers. Waiting out the ~900 s reload is the only thing that settles it on this estate, at this
version.

Output: criterion 4a and 4b evidence as dated transcripts, plus M4 and D-54's alerting or a recorded
reason M4 was deferred.
</objective>
