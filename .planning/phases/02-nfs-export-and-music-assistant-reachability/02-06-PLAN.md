<objective>
Create and configure the Music Assistant provider that reads the mounted library, and pin its
identity into the audit script so every later assertion is about *this* provider and nothing else.

Purpose: two of this phase's most expensive mistakes live in this plan's configuration, and both are
silent. `content_type` not being `music` means the `missing_album_artist_action` setting **does not
apply at all** — MA declares it with `depends_on_value="music"`. And leaving
`missing_album_artist_action` at its `various_artists` default means a track with no `albumartist`
produces a plausible-looking library entry rather than a visible failure. This library is heavy on DJ
compilations that legitimately *are* Various Artists, so the default makes the two cases
indistinguishable — the trap passes the three-album gate and detonates at Phase 7 scale.

The third silent failure is identity. MA's album identity is `albumartist + os.sep + album`, so a
wrong album artist creates a **durable** wrong-artist entry, and the cheapest repair once content has
flowed is removing and re-adding the provider, which drops every favourite and play count attached to
its items. That is the whole reason this phase exists before content flows.

Output: a configured provider, its full config captured as committed evidence, and an audit script
that can now assert against it.
</objective>
