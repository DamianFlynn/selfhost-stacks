<objective>
Build the instrument this phase is measured with, and the credentials it needs.

Purpose: `check-music-consumers.sh` is not a nicety. The operator chose to leave the Music Assistant
add-on on auto-update (D-56), which makes this script **the only mechanism that would catch an MA
release changing provider behaviour under the pipeline**. It is also the tool Phase 7's CONS-04
calls to answer "visible in both Jellyfin and Music Assistant", with the same pinned album set. Its
correctness is load-bearing well past this phase.

The three proof albums are chosen and pre-verified here, before anything can depend on them. If a bad
tag and a bad mount are both possible, an MA failure is uninterpretable — pre-verification is what
makes any later failure unambiguously the export's or MA's (D-07).

Output: `scripts/check-music-consumers.sh`, and two secrets on LXC 100 that are never committed.

This plan runs in **Wave 2, parallel with 02-02** — it touches no Terraform and needs no live export.
Its CONS-01/02/03 sections will fail until later plans land, which is exactly what `--baseline` mode
exists for.
</objective>
