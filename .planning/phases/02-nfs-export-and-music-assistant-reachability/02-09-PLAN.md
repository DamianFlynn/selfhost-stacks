<objective>
Make the phase's work durable and then close it honestly.

Purpose: three of this estate's worst incidents share one root cause — something was working, then
stopped, and nothing said anything. Dispatcharr and teleport were down six weeks. Podsync's yt-dlp was
broken for thirteen months. The docker `created`-state blind spot hid both under a green check. The
fold-in (D-41) is the answer to that for this phase, and it matters more here than it did in Phase 1
because the operator chose to leave the MA add-on on auto-update (D-56) — which makes
`check-music-consumers.sh` the only mechanism that would catch an MA release changing provider
behaviour under the pipeline.

Criterion 5 is the other half. PROJECT.md currently asserts that HA's storage configuration is the
mount route. That assertion must be **corrected**, not merely supplemented, whichever route actually
won — and the losing route's failure symptom must be named, because that is the detail a future reader
needs and the only moment it can be captured is now.

Closure is a human gate on purpose. `check-music-consumers.sh` exiting 0 is the machine verdict, but
criterion 3 is ultimately about what a person sees in Music Assistant, and this project exists because
pipelines were declared working three times and were not.

Output: standing health coverage, four updated documents, and a closure verdict.
</objective>
