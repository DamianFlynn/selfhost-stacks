<objective>
Write the contract window 3 will be judged against, and build and prove the instrument that will
produce its evidence. **This plan arms nothing, touches no container and writes no estate file.**

**Why a third design exists.** Window 1 polled the destination tree and was always too late —
SABnzbd invokes the post-processing hook *after* moving the job. Window 2 polled the `incomplete/`
tree, structurally before the move, and still failed on five real jobs across two windows
(`UNPROVEN no-attributed-pre`, `files_in_both=0` every time): under `direct_unpack=1` the audio
either never became visible to a 1-second poll or was still being written when the move happened.
Both designs sample a folder SABnzbd is actively draining. This plan stops sampling and hooks
SABnzbd's own state machine at the `pp` notification (`postproc.py:453`).

**Why the ladder is redesigned rather than transplanted, and this is the load-bearing part of this
plan.** 04-14's ladder discriminates on `pre_complete` — a property its *polling* watcher could
measure, because it kept observing the folder after publishing and could compare `pre.inv` against
the last inventory seen before the folder vanished. **A hook fires once and never re-observes.** Its
only completeness test is "did the inventory hold across my own hash pass", which is also its
publication condition — so a published snapshot always implies `pre_complete=yes`, the property is
pinned, and the clauses that depend on it are not merely untested but **unreachable**. Transplanting
the ladder unchanged would have produced two specific failures: a self-test control that cannot emit
its required status, and — worse — a clause-7 guard permanently open, so a partially-extracted album
(the `pp` event fires before `parring` at :456 and before `unpacker` at :495) would publish, have its
remaining tracks land only at COMPLETION, and be reported **`FAIL`: a tagger rewrote bytes**, against
a clean estate. The contract's own § 1 item 4 already says bytes cannot distinguish those two, and
window 2 measured the shape (job `227d31189be6`, inventory 62 → 19 mid-pass). A false FAIL is the
worst outcome this phase can produce — worse than OPEN, because 04-19 would escalate it as a genuine
finding.

Purpose: get the contract and the instrument right **before** a scarce real music job is spent, and
prove every outcome the ladder can return is reachable.

Output: § 1 item 4 and § 5 of `04-D12-EVIDENCE.md` amended, additive and dated, in one commit; and on
the host, two proven scripts plus a twelve-control receipt that plan 04-18 refuses to arm without.
</objective>
