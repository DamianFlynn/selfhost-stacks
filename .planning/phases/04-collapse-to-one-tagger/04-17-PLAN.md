**Why a third design exists at all.** Window 1 polled the destination tree and was always too late —
SABnzbd invokes the post-processing hook *after* moving the job. Window 2 polled the `incomplete/`
tree, which is structurally before the move, and still failed on five real jobs across two windows
(`STATUS=UNPROVEN reason=no-attributed-pre`, `files_in_both=0` every time): under `direct_unpack=1`
the audio either never became visible to a 1-second poll (2 of 3 jobs) or was still being written
when the move happened (1 of 3, and the instrument correctly refused the straddling pass). Both
designs share one defect — they sample a folder SABnzbd is actively draining. This plan stops
sampling and hooks SABnzbd's own state machine instead, at the `pp` notification
(`postproc.py:453`), which fires before repair, before unpack, before the move loop and before
`audio.bash`.

time — so its final task prepares, and deliberately does not sign, the operator's override.
