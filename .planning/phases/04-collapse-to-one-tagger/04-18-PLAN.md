The estate side of criterion 3 is proven clean three times over and needs nothing. Only the
measurement changes. The verdict is an **outcome, not a goal**: § 5's pre-declared rules decide, and
nothing here is satisfiable only by a PASS.

**Why restore is the terminal act of this plan and not a step behind a human checkpoint.** Arming
changes four live `sabnzbd.ini` keys and adds two paths under `/config`. `quick-health-check.sh`
carries **no `nscript_*` guard**, so an armed estate is invisible to every standing check, and a later
settings save in the SABnzbd UI would persist the change into the config SABnzbd rewrites. An
unanswered checkpoint must therefore not be able to strand the estate armed. Three independent
defences: restore is a **named idempotent script**, it is **trapped** on any non-success exit of any
task that can arm, and a **detached watchdog** runs it at a declared deadline even if this session
disappears entirely.
</objective>
