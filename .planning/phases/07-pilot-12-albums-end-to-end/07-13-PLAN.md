<objective>
Import the DJ pair the operator kept in the sample (D-04's accepted consequence) and prove the E1
routing mechanism D-08 chose on real, imported content — the step deferred three times.

Purpose: the DJ collection is working material, not an archive; the operator cannot play or build
crates from untagged tracks, and MusicBrainz carries none of the DJ fields — so a MusicBrainz-driven
import is exactly what would strip them. This plan proves they survive and that DJ content lands
under `DJ/`, never in the artist tree or `Compilations/`.

`beet move` putting it under `DJ/`, the tree is briefly wrong. No Jellyfin scan is issued inside that
window, so Jellyfin never indexes the transient paths; MA is not synced until plan 07-15.

`Full[R]efresh`. The only real-library `beet` calls are through the registered
`scripts/route-dj-album.sh` (D-27) or the UI.
