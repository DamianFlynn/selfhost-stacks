<objective>
Make `scripts/diff-music-tags.sh` — the QUAL-02 gate every pilot album passes through — fail closed
on an ambiguous join instead of silently keeping whichever duplicate record came last (D-11), and
prove the new arm both fires and stays quiet, on fixtures and on real data, before the pilot relies
on it (D-12).

Purpose: the defect is one line — `def index: reduce .[] as $r ({}; .[$r.k] = $r);` inside
`JOIN_JQ` — so for a duplicated `audio_md5` the last record in stream order wins and every earlier
one is discarded from the comparison while `dupgroups` still *reports* the count. Whether QUAL-02
fires then depends on file-walk order (E7). D-13's amendment of the `unsorted` rule (plan 07-01)
names this arm as its control, so the pilot's `unsorted` draws are only admissible while it exists.

Discretion exercised (CONTEXT § Claude's Discretion): the AMBIGUOUS shape is a `group_by(.k)` over
each side's flattened records, ambiguous where `(map(.t) | unique | length) > 1`; exit 3 sits
above exit 1 in precedence (a blind instrument outranks a measured red, matching
`phase06-oracle.sh`'s stated precedence and CONVENTIONS §1).

Output: the hardened script with a permanent 5-case `--self-test`, the CONVENTIONS §5 pin entry,
and a committed transcript of the fixture drive and the real-data reading.
</objective>
