<objective>
Prove criterion 6 in both consumers, in the carried order, and take the measurements only the
pilot's real writes can supply: D-28's direct MA reading of every `%aunique{}` firing, E6's second
measurement (D-05), and D-24's after-census.

Purpose: CONS-04 says a file is only "imported" when verified with `ffprobe` AND visible in both
Jellyfin and Music Assistant. MA's `folder_name` action fires only when folder and tag agree and
silently falls back to `Various Artists` otherwise, while its config still reads `folder_name`; an
`%aunique{}` suffix makes folder and tag differ by construction — so only a direct read is
evidence. MA is synced once, at the end, because it never purges stale entries and nothing fences
its database; any earlier sync would have captured states that were later undone or moved.

This plan edits `check-music-consumers.sh` to make D-28 and the D-05 row standing checks, so the
readings do not decay into a one-off transcript. `check-music-consumers.sh` exiting 3 is still not
a CONF-04 completion signal; no requirement box moves.
</objective>
