<objective>
Take every before-state the pilot's criteria are judged against, while nothing has yet been written:
the QUAL-01-style tag capture of the twelve source folders, their byte manifests, the D-24 entity
census, and the E6 / consumer baselines.

Purpose: criterion 4 needs a BEFORE for every pilot file; Phase 1's `pre-project` capture
(2026-08-18) predates about half of `music/` (277 files then, 531 at 06-SAMPLE's live pass) and
Phase 5's 751 in-place tag writes, so the BEFORE of record is taken here, immediately before import,
by the same instrument (`scripts/snapshot-music-tags.sh`, full `-show_format -show_streams`,
`audio_md5`-keyed). It is captured PER ALBUM because `diff-music-tags.sh` compares whole key sets:
a one-album AFTER against a twelve-album BEFORE would report eleven albums MISSING_AFTER. All twelve
folders sit under the four pinned capture roots, so D-01's mechanical argument (no root covers the
archive) is unaffected. D-24's census is one API call and is the only test the roadmap offers of
`DEF-06-45-03`'s hypothesis that ARTIST_PROOF_ROWS row 1 is an E5 problem, not an E6 one.

Nothing here writes to `/mnt/tank`, beets state, Jellyfin or Music Assistant.
</objective>
