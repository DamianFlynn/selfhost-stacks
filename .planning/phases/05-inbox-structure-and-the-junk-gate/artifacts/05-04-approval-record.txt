HOW THE AMENDMENT WAS APPLIED
-----------------------------

can be mistyped, and every row here names something that will be destroyed:

  awk -F'\t' 'BEGIN{OFS="\t"}
    $2 ~ /\/\.covers$/ { next }
    $1 == "R1"         { $7 = "99-quarantine"; print; next }
                       { print }'

the as-read and amended files shows exactly two changes and nothing else: eight `.covers`
lines deleted, two R1 lines gaining a seventh field. Nothing was added, widened or reworded.
