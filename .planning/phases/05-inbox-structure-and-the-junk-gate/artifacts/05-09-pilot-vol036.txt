---------------------------------------------------
The plan text (05-09-PLAN.md, Task 1) nominates `Vol 077` as the pilot. The operator
substituted `Vol 036` when approving the write. Recorded here rather than silently followed,
because the substitution is load-bearing:

far as it goes, but it corroborates the TRACK COUNT, not the album string, and it is the album
string this plan writes.

Consequence for the pre-declared numbers: the plan's "44 files changed" becomes 40 files seen /
36 changed / 4 no-op, and "MATCHED 44" becomes MATCHED 40. The SHAPE of every assertion is

7. WHY `MATCHED 40` IS THE LOAD-BEARING NUMBER
-----------------------------------------------
MATCHED 40 is a DIRECT MEASUREMENT that `audio_md5` did not move on a single one of the 36
written files. It is not an inference from one.

`diff-music-tags.sh` joins the two captures on `audio_md5`, the md5 of the ENCODED AUDIO
BITSTREAM (`ffmpeg -map 0:a -c copy -f md5 -`). If `preserve_id3v1_trailer()` had failed to hold,
`mutagen.id3.ID3.save(v1=UPDATE)` would have regenerated all 128 bytes of the trailing ID3v1
block from the ID3v2 frames — and for these files ffmpeg's mp3 demuxer emits those trailing bytes
as part of the copied stream. The key would have moved, and the 36 files would have appeared as
36 MISSING_AFTER plus 36 NEW_AFTER instead of falling inside MATCHED 40.

That is not a hypothetical: plan 05-08 drove exactly this failure from a mutated copy of the tool
with `preserve_id3v1_trailer()` neutered, and caught the regeneration in the act. Phase 4 measured
it on 37 of 335 real files. D-10's entire safety argument — "writing a tag does not break Phase
7's diff join" — rests on this property, and it has now been measured on real Phase 5 files
rather than restated.

The vacuous-pass guard is the companion figure and it is stated explicitly: ZERO matched files had
an empty tag set on the BEFORE side (05-07 measured min 15 / mean 20.7 / max 30 tag fields per
file). "No fields dropped" here means fields were there and survived, not that there was nothing
to drop.
