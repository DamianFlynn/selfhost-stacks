engine — hard-errors **only** on multi-disc releases. v0.20.0's `Validate` constructs single-medium
synthetic releases only, which is exactly why the repo's broken format passed startup validation for
eight months. **Demonstrate the inversion; do not assert it, and do not repeat the wrong version.**
