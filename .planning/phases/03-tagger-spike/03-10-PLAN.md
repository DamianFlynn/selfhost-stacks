<objective>
Score axis two — the front end — by putting the same five albums through a raw terminal prompt and
through a reviewable queue, timed, with the operator personally at the keyboard.

This is the axis that may decide whether the project finishes. The operator's first-hand experience
is that beets has been painful to get working, and **a painful tool is the documented cause of all
three prior abandonments**. Criterion 6 is explicit that a decision record answering only axis one
has not met TAGR-06.

**D-09:** ergonomics is measured by a timed hands-on trial run by the operator personally, not
predicted from documentation. The roadmap's own overturning threshold — T3, *"an honest
self-assessment that the interactive prompt will not be sat at"* — can only be produced by sitting
at it. **D-10** fixes the set at 5 albums: 2 clean mainstream, 2 hard shapes (one various-artist
compilation, one flat multi-disc set), and 1 DJ folder Discogs will not match. Ergonomic pain
appears on ambiguous matches, not on the happy path; the no-match case measures what happens when
the tool *cannot* help, which is where a front end either saves the operator or loses them.

**D-11 / criterion 7:** beets-flask is evaluated **by name**, not dismissed and not assumed, with
its known frictions recorded as part of the evaluation rather than discovered in Phase 7.

**OD-2** applies here: this arm runs beets **2.12.0**, hard-pinned by rc6, while the terminal arm
runs 2.13.1. Axis two is scored on interaction, not match quality.

Purpose: TAGR-06 and criteria 6, 7 and 8.
Output: a throwaway beets-flask instance, a filled scoring sheet, `03-BEETS-FLASK.md`, and T3's
verdict.
