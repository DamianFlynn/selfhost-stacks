alongside its un-normalised pair.

**OD-4 corrects D-08's worked example and leaves its constraint intact.** D-08 says the script
principally repairs swapped `artist`/`album`. All 764 files in Phase 1's ffprobe fence were measured
and **that defect is not present**: `album` carries a correct-ish issue string in 657 of 764 files;
`artist` is a placeholder (`Mastermix` 197, `VA` 160, `Various Artists` 138, missing 77, `Various`

no query whatsoever. The real defect is sharper and more tractable, and the three measured rules
replace the example. D-08's constraint stands unchanged: minimum viable, `--dry-run` mode, committed
under `scripts/`, and **explicitly not the DJCC-01 field-mapping tool** — that is v2 and the
lowest-confidence research area in the project.

impossible on the host and cannot be made possible without a host package install. mutagen ships in
the LSIO beets image as a beets dependency. The file still lives under `scripts/` in the repo and is
mounted in read-only.

Purpose: beets builds its Discogs query from the existing `artist` and `album` tags. A rate measured
on raw tags under-reports and could wrongly disqualify the winner — that is criterion 1's entire
point.
Output: `scripts/normalise-dj-tags.py`, a reviewed dry-run diff, `normalised/` written in place, and
a `diff-music-tags.sh` verdict showing only `album` and `artist` moved.
