6. TWO INSTRUMENT DEFECTS, AND THE SEVENTH PREDICATE-CANNOT-MATCH BUG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Driver 1 exited 1. Neither cause was in the configuration under test, in the
inbox, or in the estate. Both are in the instrument, and both are the shape this
phase keeps producing: a confident-looking answer about something other than
what it claimed to measure.

  D2 — THE SEVENTH PREDICATE THAT COULD NOT MATCH.
    The poll loop broke on the literal `"progress": 20`. But the two instruments
    that report progress DISAGREE ABOUT THE REPRESENTATION:
        sqlite `session.progress`        ->  'PREVIEW_COMPLETED'   (the enum NAME)
        GET /api_v1/session/             ->  20                    (the enum VALUE)
    Both forms are visible side by side in § TRANSCRIPT RUN 1 (steps 4 and 6) —
    the same session, the same instant, two different renderings. The loop read
    the sqlite form, so it could never match, spun out its full 180 s bound and
    printed
        ">>> COULD NOT LOOK: the session did not reach PREVIEW_COMPLETED within 180 s"
    which was FALSE: the transcript shows it reached PREVIEW_COMPLETED at
    08:31:06Z, on the SECOND poll, 48 s after the copy.
    ⚠ Note the failure mode: this one failed LOUDLY, as a could-not-look, only
      because the wait had a bound and the loop printed every poll. Written as a
      bare `if done: pass` it would have been a silent green.
    FIX: `is_done()` accepts the enum name, the integer and the integer-as-string,
    and the fix is DRIVEN AGAINST PLANTED POSITIVES before any clean result from
    it is believed — six planted values that must NOT pass, including
    `LOOKING_UP_CANDIDATES` (mid-flight), `OFFERING_MATCHES` (an ImportSession
    state) and `IMPORT_COMPLETED` (an import finished, which must never read as
    a preview pass). All ten cases pass; see § TRANSCRIPT RUN 2 step 1.

  D1 — A CRASH REPORTED AS AN EMPTY RESULT.
    `items.fixed_values["path"]` is not a string. It is
        {"__type__": "bytes", "data": "<base64>"}
    `os.path.basename()` on it raises `TypeError: expected str, bytes or
    os.PathLike object, not dict`. The driver caught the non-zero exit and
    printed
        ">>> COULD NOT LOOK: the comparison could not be made"
    while the compare script's own exit-1 branch is labelled "arm 2 produced no
    items" — and arm 2 had produced all 15. A wrong CAUSE attached to a real
    failure is how the next reader concludes the preview found nothing.
    FIX: `decode_path()` handles str, bytes and the dict encoding, REFUSES
    anything else with a TypeError rather than guessing, and is self-tested both
    ways. The compare script now distinguishes "the extractor raised" from "the
    extractor ran and found nothing", and reports the exception text in the
    verdict line.

  HOW THE FIX WAS VERIFIED WITHOUT A THIRD TRANSIT.
    beets-flask's `folder`, `session`, `task` and `items` rows OUTLIVE the
    directory they describe — plan 06-06 recorded exactly that as its
    dangling-state finding. So the fixed instrument was re-run against the state
    the single run-1 transit had already persisted. `02-review` was empty for the
    whole of run 2 and is asserted so in its own transcript. No folder was
    staged twice, and the amendment in § 5 stands at two transits, not three.
