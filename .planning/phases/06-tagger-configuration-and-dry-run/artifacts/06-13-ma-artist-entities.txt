SECTION 1 — TASK 1: THE D-36 OPERATOR GATE

WHY THIS GATE EXISTS
  D-36 makes the MA arm a stop-and-ask, NOT a retry loop. At research time
  (2026-09-20T03:26Z, measured twice) `172.16.1.31` answered ICMP while TCP 8095
  (Music Assistant) and 8123 (Home Assistant) were both CLOSED — because the host
  was down for maintenance, which is the operator's own statement and not an
  inference drawn from a probe. A check run blind against a down service produces
  a red that means "could not look", and this estate's standing rule
  (README § Health Checks) is that "could not look" must never be folded into a
  verdict. The host being reachable is also NOT the same thing as the operator
  being ready for MA to be exercised, which is why a green probe alone does not
  authorise task 2.
