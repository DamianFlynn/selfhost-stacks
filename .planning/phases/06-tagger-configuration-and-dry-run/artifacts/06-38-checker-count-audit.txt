SCOPE, STATED TIGHTLY. An unbounded audit is how a two-task plan becomes a

  IN SCOPE:  one file, scripts/check-beets-config.sh; and one class of claim --
             an in-band statement of HOW MANY TIMES some token appears in this

  OUT OF SCOPE: claims about beets, about the estate, about other scripts'

ENUMERATION METHOD
  1. A mechanical sweep over comment lines carrying a digit alongside a count
     word:
        /usr/bin/grep -n '^[[:space:]]*#' scripts/check-beets-config.sh \
          | /usr/bin/grep -iE '[0-9]' \
          | /usr/bin/grep -iE 'hit|occurrence|times|count|returns|lines|exactly|only|both|two|three|four|one '
  2. Then a full end-to-end read of the file, because a claim phrased without a
     keyword ("the definition and the one call") is invisible to step 1. A
     targeted read is precisely what let this class survive three rounds.

KNOWN GAPS IN THAT METHOD -- THIS IS NOT A COMPLETENESS PROOF.
  - Step 1 keys on an English keyword list that is not closed. A claim phrased
    with a word not in the list, and not caught by the read in step 2, is missed.
  - A count claim written as a spelled-out number with no digit ("twice", "both
    arms") is invisible to step 1 and depends entirely on the human read.
  - The read in step 2 is one pass by one reader. Three consecutive review rounds
    have found an instance of this class that the previous round's reader missed,
    so the honest prior is that this pass is not exhaustive either.
  - The audit measures claims AS OF this plan's HEAD. Any claim dispositioned
    ACCURATE AND STABLE is stable against edits to THIS file only.
