================================================================================

While writing the `beets.md` note for this very plan, the explanatory sentence

    > a **bare** `"$BEET" config`, so it cannot be accused of only matching …

was added to the runbook. It is prose. It is also, character for character, an
invocation shape under the branch this plan just wrote: a quote, a BEET-prefixed
variable, a closing quote, whitespace, a lowercase subcommand word. The widened
pattern saw it immediately:

    before the sentence:  raw=191  stripped=90  shaped=10  doc=2  exe=8
    with the sentence:    raw=200  stripped=99  shaped=11  doc=3  exe=8
                                                           ^^^^^
    the new documentation hit:
      HEAD:stacks/selfhosted/arrs/beets.md:2204:> a **bare** `"$BEET" config`, so it cannot be accused of only matching invocations that were
    after rephrasing:     raw=199  stripped=98  shaped=10  doc=2  exe=8

`D04_DOC_BASELINE` is pinned at 2, so `doc=3` is a RED. The sentence would have
turned the runbook red the moment the tree reached the host.

TWO THINGS THIS IS EVIDENCE OF, and they are worth more than a planned control:

  1. THE DETECTOR WORKS ON UNPLANNED INPUT. Nobody wrote this line to be caught.
     It was ordinary prose describing the fix, and the fix caught it. The
     equivalent sentence would have sailed past the 2026-09-21 pattern without a
     flicker, because that pattern could not see a `$BEET` variable at all.
  2. THE DOCUMENTATION PIN IS NOT DECORATION. Its stated purpose — "a new
     copy-pasteable bare invocation in the runbook is a real footgun" — is
     exactly what fired. The line WAS copy-pasteable and WAS bare.

WHAT WAS DONE, AND WHY IT IS NOT THE SAME AS REWRITING EVIDENCE. The sentence was
REPHRASED and `D04_DOC_BASELINE` was LEFT AT 2. Raising the pin to 3 would have
been laundering: the pin exists to catch precisely this, and moving it to
accommodate the first thing it caught would retire the check on its first use.
The distinction from the two historic quotations at `beets.md:106` and `:170`,
which ARE kept verbatim, is that those are the Nov-2025 RECORD of what the
runbook said — rewriting them would falsify history. This sentence was written
today, by this plan, and is not a record of anything. Quoting is not free; a
quotation earns its baseline slot by being evidence.

HONEST LIMIT ON THIS DRIVE: the count movement (2 -> 3 -> 2) was observed through
the probe that extracts `D04_INV_RE` and `D04_EXEMPT_RE` FROM the script and
applies them to a real `git grep`, not through a captured live run of
