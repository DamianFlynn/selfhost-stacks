FINDING 2 — THE PREDICATE HAD TO BE WRITTEN THE OTHER WAY ROUND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the sixth time this phase that a predicate which cannot match read as a
clean result, and it was caught before it was believed.

`Distance.add_priority()` assigns `index * (1/len(options))`, i.e. **0.0** to a
value matching the FIRST entry, and 1.0 when nothing matches
(beets/autotag/hooks.py@v2.12.0). `Distance.items()` then — quoting its own
docstring — "Does not include penalties with a zero value."

So the chosen GB candidate has NO `country` key in its penalty breakdown, and a
predicate reading "the chosen candidate carries a country penalty" would have
gone RED **because the preference worked perfectly**. The correct assertion is
the inverse, and both halves are asserted:

  * the chosen GB candidate carries NO country penalty  -> it matched at index 0
  * all 4 non-GB candidates DO carry one                -> 4 of 4

FINDING 8 — `musicbrainz.extra_tags` REACHED THE WIRE, AND TWO OF FIVE DID NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Read from the plugin's own query builder (`get_search_query_with_filters`),
not inferred from the config value.

  declared           ['year', 'catalognum', 'country', 'media', 'label']
  mapped to MB keys  year->date  catalognum->catno  country->country
                     media->format  label->label          (5 of 5 mapped)
  ACTUALLY SENT      {"release":"now that's what i call music! 1",
                      "arid":"89ad4ac3-39f7-470e-963a-56509c546377",
                      "date":"2018", "catno":"cdnow01", "format":"cd"}
  dropped            country, label

  `get_search_query_with_filters` drops any criterion whose value is empty after
  `.lower().strip()`. `country` and `label` are dropped because beets reads both
  as the empty string on this content: the files carry `TXXX:COUNTRY` and
  `TXXX:ORGANIZATION`, neither of which is a mediafile field, rather than the
  standard frames. So three of the five extra tags are doing work here and two
  are inert *on this content* — not misconfigured, just unfed.

  And they matter: `date=2018` plus `catno=cdnow01` is why the 2018 GB reissue
  family dominates the result set at all.

FINDING 9 — `preferred.original_year` IS IN FORCE BUT DOES NOT DECIDE THIS ROW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * The chosen candidate has year **2018** and original_year **1983** — they
    differ, so the `original_year` branch of `distance()` is the one taken.
  * It carries a `year` penalty of 0.012149. With `preferred.original_year: yes`
    the penalty is `|year − original| / |today − original|` = |2018−1983| /
    |2026−1983| = 35/43 = 0.814 raw. So the setting is measurably active — and
    note it PENALISES the reissue the sample actually holds.
  * A prediction made before the run — that turning it off would flip rank 0
    between the two 30-track GB candidates (2009 and 2018 pressings) — was
    WRONG and is recorded as wrong. On the widened pool, `original_year: yes`
    (D) and `original_year: no` (H) both choose
    `07971e8a-9b1a-31a0-a111-f69955974138` (GB, 2009, original 1983). The key is
    exercised; it does not change the outcome for this row.
