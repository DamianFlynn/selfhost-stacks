<objective>
Close **GC-02** and **GC-06**, the two round-2 Warnings in `scripts/phase06-oracle.sh`.

GC-02: the **sending-side** fence restricts the path suffix to a character class, and its own
comment says why — *"`/tmp/p6-*` on its own would still admit `/tmp/p6-x /config`, which is the
very shape the fence exists to refuse."* The **receiving-side** fence, the one adjacent to the
`rm -rf`, is exactly that bare glob. `[A-Za-z0-9._-]` excludes `/`; the glob does not. So the inner
fence accepts `/tmp/p6-x/../../../home` and `/mnt/fast/safety/phase06/../../../../etc/shadow` and
would run `rm -rf` / `rm -f` on them. That is a finding rather than a nit because the inner fence is
documented at three sites as the layer whose *purpose* is to survive the loss of the outer one —
*"the sending layer stops the common case; this one is the one that cannot be bypassed by a future
edit that forgets the first."* **That claim is false as written.** The two copies the WR-07 brief
asked to be checked for drift had already drifted, at birth, in the direction that matters. The next
reader who deletes the outer fence as "duplicated" gets a path-traversal-capable `rm -rf` and a
comment telling them it is covered.

It is not a BLOCKER, and the plan must not overstate it: as shipped, the outer fence runs before the
first ssh of every mode, `remote_sh_c` passes the path as a positional parameter so nothing can
re-split it in transit, and `"$1"` is quoted at the `rm`. No current invocation reaches the weak
fence with a hostile value.

GC-06: plan 06-19 introduced `st_grep_why` explicitly to avoid `printf | grep -q`, with a comment
saying *"that shape has produced a false red in three plans of this phase already."* Plan 06-18's
WR-08 self-test section, 300 lines above it, uses the shape it warns about, in three places. Two of
them fail in the safe direction (a 141 would be a false red). **The third fails in the unsafe
direction:** it is an inverted assertion — *the old interpolated shape must NOT be present* — so a
grep **match** is the failure case, which is exactly when `grep -q` exits early and can SIGPIPE
`printf`. A 141 makes the `if` false, `rc` stays 0, and the case reports **passed** at the precise
moment it should have failed. An assertion that can silently not fire is this phase's dominant
defect class, and it is the same mechanism as GC-01 pointed the dangerous way round.

Output: three receiving-side fences with the sending side's exact predicate, a self-test case that
drives a traversal path against the inner fence with the outer one absent, and a self-test section
with no pipeline carrying a verdict.
</objective>
