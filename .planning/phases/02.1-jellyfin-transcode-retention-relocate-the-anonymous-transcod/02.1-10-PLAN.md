<objective>
Turn a one-time proof into standing coverage, prove the coverage fails closed, and record what is not
covered.

Phases 1 and 2 both closed by folding a check into `scripts/quick-health-check.sh` (D-29, D-41). D-20
continues that. It is the difference between a phase that fixed something and a phase that will notice
when the fix comes undone — and this phase exists precisely because nothing noticed for 36 hours while
`/` went to zero.

Three things this plan does that the fold-in itself does not:

**It executes the negative controls.** D-19 says an unreachable LXC 100, an unreachable atlantis, a
stopped Jellyfin and an unmounted dataset are all **failures**, not skips — the convention
`check-music-consumers.sh` already uses, and the reason it exists is that the estate's worst outage was
six weeks of silence being read as clean. Reading the fail-closed branches and agreeing they look right
does not discharge VALIDATION.md rows 21-24.

**It restores everything it breaks, and proves the restore.** The cross-AI review found that the
exit-code-propagation test required temporarily editing `quick-health-check.sh` — because the fold-in's
ssh command is hardcoded — but, unlike the anchor-break test, had **no stated restore-and-reverify
step**. This plan removes the need for that edit entirely and states the restore discipline for every
control that touches anything.

**It records what is not covered, where the reader will be standing.** D-22 keeps `quick-health-check.sh`
**manual** and requires the gap be written down. And both reviewers independently said the Live TV gap
and the volume inventory belong in `jellyfin.yaml` rather than `PROJECT.md`'s Key Decisions table — a
Music Library Consolidation document already around 240 lines. Plan 02.1-04 already put them there; this
plan verifies that rather than duplicating them, and keeps only the genuinely estate-wide limits in
PROJECT.md.

The fold-in's own trap is already documented twice: the WR-09 defect, where the consuming block anchors
on `^📊 N\. Summary` and a renumbering silently breaks it while the branch that prints a tick still
runs. Both existing fold-ins now treat a missing anchor as UNKNOWN rather than green. The third must
too, and this plan proves it by breaking the anchor on purpose — and putting it back.

Purpose: TRAN-05's standing, fail-closed detection — the thing that would have caught the incident
before `/` reached zero.
Output: a third fold-in block, six executed controls with their restores, and the known limits on
record.
