HOW TO TELL A RESTART FROM A FAILURE — stated BEFORE the operator looks:

  RESTART (expected, correct):
    - a brief buffering spinner, typically a few seconds
    - playback resumes AT THE POSITION SOUGHT
    - the Dashboard session still shows a transcode, progress reset to the new position
    - nothing surfaces to the user beyond the pause

  FAILURE:
    - an error dialog or toast ("Playback failed", "No compatible streams",
      "This client isn't compatible")
    - the player drops back to the item detail page
    - the spinner never clears — give it 60 SECONDS, past which it is a failure and not a
      slow restart
    - video without audio, or audio without video
