## Contents of TAILSCALE.md

1. Tailnet identity — `pirate-clownfish.ts.net`, and the tagged-vs-user-owned key expiry split
2. Topology diagram
3. **The four deployment patterns** — `tailscale-udm` on the UCG-Max, the HAOS add-on, `tsbridge`
   OAuth per-service nodes, stock clients. Each with versions, paths and config tables
4. Exit nodes (two, opt-in per client)
5. Subnet routing, failover (~30 s measured, **sticky, does not fail back**), and the
   standby LAN-IP behaviour documented as expected behaviour with its workaround
6. The SNAT asymmetry between the two routers and why source-IP filters must tolerate both
7. Failure modes — firmware wipe + OOM, the former single-router SPOF, key expiry, and the
   IPTV `max_streams=1` interaction that makes failover tests disruptive
8. Runbook — recover a dead gateway client, verify, fail back, add a service, onboard a router
9. Open items
